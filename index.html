Time Vs Authenticity

While an object's age is an important factor, authenticity is the most important element in determining true value. A real antique is an authentic piece, crafted in the era it represents using genuine tools and methods of that time.

Authenticity ultimately determines the actual value of a piece. Any object preserved in its original condition is much more valuable than a piece that has been altered. Refinishing, repairing, or replacing parts of an antique significantly lowers its historical and intrinsic value.

1616 LEBBO COIN

Historical Copper Iridium coins of profound significance and unique properties.

Mystical Properties

Copper Iridium coins are known for their unique properties. According to history, they show remarkable magnetic and elemental phenomena.

Facts about the 1616 Coin

In 1603 AD, the East India Company was formed with establishments across India. By the year 1616 AD, during a complete solar eclipse that lasted for more than 5 hours, specialized Copper coins infused with Copper Iridium were minted. These coins were entirely hand-made in assorted weights and sizes. They represent a unique mix of history, astronomical events, and ancient metallurgy.

OUR PROCEDURE

A rigorous, transparent process of documentation and execution designed to ensure absolute authenticity and security for all parties involved.

Declaration Requirements

Each and every beneficiary must submit complete verification documentation before proceeding.

  • Ten passport size photographs
  • Photo Identity Card like Voters ID, Pan Card, or Driving License
  • Residential Address Proof for documentation, banking, and taxation formalities

Also, exact spot address details, distance from a major Tier 1 city, and Police Station Jurisdiction must be clearly given prior to testing.

Terms & Conditions

  • A non-refundable security deposit is required in the company account prior to testing.
  • Testing locations must be secure, accessible by motorable roads, and within a precise radius approved by the company.
  • Only two representatives from the owner's side may be present to ensure an uninterrupted testing process.
  • A finance bond will be issued, valid for 12 hours. If testing fails or material isn't produced, the bond expires.
  • After successful certification, staggered bank-to-bank payments will be initiated over the next working days. Complete documentation takes approximately 15 to 25 days.

Testing Procedures

Depending on the specific rare material, our qualified metallurgists will perform a series of tests.

  • Copper Iridium: Rice pulling tests, water tests, and localized electronic discharge tests.
  • Corbosol: Touch tests, flash tests, sound reaction, and rainbow visual tests.
  • Anti-Mirror / Shadow: Visibility and shadow directional tests.
  • Herbals: Gauge wire cutting speeds and natural healing observations.
