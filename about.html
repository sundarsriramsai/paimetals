ABOUT PAI METALS

PAI METALS is a Europe based company delivering trusted rare metal sourcing, article verification and technical testing services worldwide.

Company Profile

PAI METALS aims to provide timely, economic, and important industry information for the benefit of our customers. We offer networking opportunities in the global market, bringing together producers, refiners, fabricators, scientists, and financial institutions.

We are a central forum for the exchange of information and technology within the precious metals community, promoting efficient and environmentally sound use, reuse, and recycling of materials. We are fully committed to serving our customers with uncompromising integrity, professionalism, and expertise.
